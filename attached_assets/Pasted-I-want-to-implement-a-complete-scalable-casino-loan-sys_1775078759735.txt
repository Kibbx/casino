1. CREDIT SCORE SYSTEM

---

Each player has a creditScore (0–1000) based on behavior.

Track metrics:

* lifetimeDeposits
* totalWagered
* loansRepaid
* defaults
* overdueLoans
* activeDays

Formula:

score =
(Math.sqrt(lifetimeDeposits) * 0.5) +
(Math.sqrt(totalWagered) * 0.4) +
(loansRepaid * 35) +
(activeDays * 5)

* (defaults * 100)
* (overdueLoans * 50)

Clamp between 0 and 1000.

Rules:

* Require at least 3 activeDays AND 50k totalWagered before loans allowed
* Update score automatically on:

  * deposits
  * wagers
  * loan repayment
  * overdue/default events

Loan rules:

* totalOwed = principal + interest (flat OR daily depending on settings)
* remainingBalance decreases with payments
* Loan creation adds chips to player balance
* All actions log transactions:

  * "Loan issued"
  * "Loan repayment"
  * "Interest applied"

Loan limits:

maxLoan = creditScore * loanMultiplier (from settings)

Interest rate:

interestRate = baseRate - (creditScore / 1000 * scaling)
