Add an odds format switch directly in the Bet Slip header, positioned next to the "Clear" button, allowing users to instantly toggle between American and European (Decimal) odds.

Requirements:
- Place a compact toggle/switch beside the existing "Clear" button in the Bet Slip header.
- The toggle should have two options:
  - US (American Odds)
  - EU (Decimal Odds)
- The switch should match the existing premium dark sportsbook theme with subtle animations and neon accents.
- It should feel like a native part of the Bet Slip, not a separate settings panel.

Functionality:
- Switching formats should update all displayed odds instantly without refreshing the page.
- Update odds everywhere in the sportsbook:
  - Event cards
  - Bet Slip selections
  - Combined Parlay Odds
  - Cash Out values
  - Open Bets
  - Bet History
  - Live Bets
  - Any future sportsbook odds displays
- The selected format should persist across page refreshes and future sessions for the logged-in user.
- Save the preference in the backend user settings (or local storage if user settings aren't available).

Backend:
- Store one canonical odds format internally (prefer Decimal for calculations).
- Convert odds only when displaying them to the user.
- Ensure all payout calculations continue using the canonical values.
- Do not change sportsbook pricing or calculations when switching formats.

Conversions:
- American → Decimal
- Decimal → American
- Correctly calculate positive and negative American odds.
- Round decimal odds to two decimal places.
- Display American odds with + and - signs.

UI:
- Keep the Bet Slip exactly the same size.
- The toggle should fit neatly beside the "Clear" button without crowding the header.
- Use a compact pill-style segmented switch (US | EU) with a smooth animated slider.
- Highlight the active selection with the sportsbook's orange accent color.
- Maintain full responsiveness for desktop and mobile.

Important:
This is a display preference only. Switching formats must never alter the actual odds, payouts, or sportsbook calculations—only how they are presented to the user.
