Upgrade Slot Tournaments to support ROLLING ENTRY (players can join at any time until the tournament ends).

GOAL:
Allow players to enter slot tournaments at any time during the active window, instead of requiring all players to join before start.

---

1. TOURNAMENT STATE FLOW (UPDATED)

---

Slot tournaments now have:

status:

* "active" (immediately upon creation)
* "ended"

REMOVE:

* Waiting/registration-only phase for slots

IMPORTANT:

* Tournament starts immediately when created
* Players can join anytime while status = "active"

---

2. CREATE TOURNAMENT (SLOTS)

---

When creating a slot tournament:

SET:

* status = "active"

ADD:

* duration (required)
  e.g. 10 min / 30 min / 1 hour

* endTime = createdAt + duration

---

3. ENTRY RULES

---

Players can join IF:

* currentTime < endTime
* player has enough chips for buy-in

On join:

* Deduct buy-in
* Initialize:
  tournamentChips
  score = 0
  biggestSpin = 0

---

4. FAIRNESS HANDLING (IMPORTANT)

---

Each player gets:

* FULL starting tournamentChips regardless of join time

BUT:

* Less time to use them if joining late

This creates:

* Early join = more time advantage
* Late join = high-risk, fast play

---

5. TOURNAMENT END

---

When:
currentTime >= endTime

* Tournament status = "ended"
* Lock all spins
* Determine winner by highest score

---

6. UI CHANGES (CRITICAL)

---

On tournament card + page show:

⏳ Time Remaining: 12:34

* Always visible countdown

---

7. JOIN UX

---

Display:

* "Join Now" button (while active)
* Show:

  * Prize pool
  * Current top score
  * Players entered

---

8. LEADERBOARD (LIVE)

---

* Update continuously
* Players see:

  * Current leader
  * Their rank
  * Score gap

---

9. ANTI-ABUSE / EDGE CASES

---

* Prevent joining after endTime
* Lock player actions instantly at endTime
* Do NOT allow re-entry unless explicitly enabled

---

10. OPTIONAL (HIGHLY RECOMMENDED)

---

Add:

"Late Entry Warning"

If joining with <30% time left:
→ Show warning:
"⚠️ Limited time remaining — high risk entry"

---

11. KEEP CORE SYSTEM

---

* Tournament chips system (chips go down only)
* Score system (winnings only)
* Variable betting
* Biggest spin tracking

---

12. DO NOT BREAK

---

* Poker tournaments (still scheduled)
* Slot logic
* Economy system

---

## RESULT:

* No waiting for players
* Constant tournament availability
* More participation
* Better player retention
* Natural urgency via countdown timer
